Scrap Reference

When an enemy is defeated it drops either scrap or one piece of its equipment — never both. There is a 90% chance of dropping scrap, where the rarity tier matches the enemy's rarity. There is a 10% chance of dropping one equipment item from its loadout instead, with rarer equipment being less likely to drop.
